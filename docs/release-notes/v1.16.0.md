## New: Stealer guard
Password stealers (RedLine, Lumma, fake "Roblox executors", "free Nitro" generators, malicious Minecraft mods) grab your saved logins within seconds of starting. Sentinel already catches the known ones by fingerprint. Stealer guard catches new ones by what they do:

- **Programs opening your logins:** new programs that aren't signed by a publisher (and script runners like Python, PowerShell and Java) are checked while they run. If one opens your saved browser passwords or cookies, Discord, Roblox, Steam or Minecraft login, Telegram or crypto wallets, **Sentinel pauses it straight away** and asks: **End and quarantine**, or **Allow**.
- **Stolen data being collected:** Sentinel watches Temp and AppData for copies of your browser passwords and cookies, Discord tokens, Roblox sign-in cookies and password lists, the way stealers gather data before sending it.
- The recovery checklist opens automatically, since some data may already be gone.

Your browsers and apps reading their own data never count. Find it in **Tools → Stealer guard**, with the last warnings, programs you allowed, and an on/off switch. The weekly report now counts these warnings too.

## Upgrading
If you're on 1.13.0 or later, this installs automatically in the background. Otherwise, open the **Updates** tab and click **Update now**, or run **SentinelSetup.exe** below over your existing install.

The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will warn you. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
